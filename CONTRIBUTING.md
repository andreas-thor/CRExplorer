# Contributing to CRExplorer

Thank you for contributing to CRExplorer.

Unless you explicitly state otherwise, any contribution intentionally submitted
for inclusion in CRExplorer is provided under the Apache License 2.0, in
accordance with section 5 of that license.

By submitting a contribution, you represent that you have the right to submit
it under these terms. Do not submit code, documentation, data, images, or other
material copied from a third party unless its license permits inclusion and the
original license and attribution information are preserved.

Clearly identify all third-party material and its source and license in the pull
request. If a contribution was created within an employment or service
relationship, confirm that you have any authorization required from the relevant
employer or institution.
